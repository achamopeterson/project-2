Why We Created This Site

We created this website to look at technology from more than just the technical side. Technology is part of our everyday lives, but people can experience the same technology in different ways. Through this site, we want to explore how identity, implicit bias, and intersectionality can affect the way technology is created and experienced.

Our goal is to make these ideas easier to understand, especially for students who may be learning about them for the first time.

What we Hope You Learn

We hope this website helps visitors understand that technology is connected to people and society. By learning more about implicit bias and intersectionality, we can think more carefully about who technology is designed for and how it can be made more inclusive.
