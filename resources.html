What is Implicit Bias?

One way of looking at implicit bias is that it is made up of opinions or associations that we hold without necessarily being aware of it. In the test for implicit bias, we were asked to categorize various words quickly. The process was easier for some groupings than others. This caused us to realize we subconsciously made choices prior to even taking all information into account.

Our Experience with the Implicit Bias Test

One thing that really seemed to stick out to us is how quickly we had to respond when taking the implicit bias test. We were hardly given any time to think over our answers. Some of the combinations felt easy to answer meanwhile, some of the others made us realize how biased ones mind can be without even realzing. Before taking the test, we mostly thought of bias as treating someone differently because of things like their race, gender, or background. The test helped us realize that bias is much more complicated and is a much more complex topic.

Understanding Intersectionality

Intersectionality is the idea that multiple aspects of an individual's identity can overlap and affect one's experiences, such as race and gender. During our project #1 we discussed how people should not be judged by their given labels because everyone has their own real life experiences and personalities that make everyone different from just a stereotype. We think understanding intersectionality is important because it reminds us to be more open minded.
